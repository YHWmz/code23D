# 艺术宫外部空间｜客户沟通与交付说明

## 项目边界
| 项目 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| 使用场景 | 设计师向业主沟通艺术宫外部空间装修方案，材质和空间关系需要直观可辨 |
| 场地限制 | 现有场地布局保持不变 |
| 参考方式 | 配图只作为布局、构图、风格及氛围参照，制作相关的新设计，不做逐像素复刻 |
| 补充资料 | 可以查找同一真实场景的其他公开参考，用于校对建筑结构、尺度和材质表现 |
| 人物处理 | 忽略配图中的人物，不作为建模对象 |

## 场景完整度与表现
- 主体建筑的多层结构、装饰细节、周边环境及常规陈设都要完整制作，尺度与材质响应符合现实。
- 先保证整体布局开阔、宏大，再打磨会进入近景的建筑、水面和环境细节。
- 建筑表面、水体与绿化之间需要清楚区分，方便业主看方案时判断用材和空间层次。
- 天气与配图一致：晴朗蓝天、零散白云和清晰自然日光；阴影、水面反射及高光应真实可信。

## 漫游镜头
- 时长：10秒。
- 开场采用开阔的整体视野，让主要立面完整入画并保持主体无遮挡。
- 随后向近景细节推进，形成连续的场景穿梭体验；镜头节奏和具体路径可自行把握。

## 交付规格
| 项目 | 参数 |
|---|---|
| 成片格式 | MP4 |
| 分辨率 | 1080p |
| 帧率 | 24fps |
| 输出内容 | 最终动画渲染成片 |
| 工程文件 | 保留可继续调整的 `.blend` 工程 |

# English handoff

I’m the designer presenting an exterior refurbishment concept for an art palace to the client. The existing site plan is locked. Use the supplied image as a reference for layout, composition, style and atmosphere, but develop a related new design rather than a literal copy. Additional public references of the real location may be used to verify structure, scale and material response. Ignore all people in the image.

The complete architectural hierarchy, decorative detail, surrounding environment and normal site dressing should be present and physically believable. Establish the broad, monumental layout first, then refine anything that comes close to camera. Materials must read distinctly for the client, with convincing daylight, shadows, water reflections and highlights matching the clear blue sky and scattered-cloud conditions in the reference.

The priority is to keep the main facade fully visible in the establishing view and make sure the subject is never obscured by incidental clutter. The fly-through should then move toward closer details; use your judgment on the exact path and pacing.

Delivery is a 10-second rendered MP4 at 1080p and 24fps, together with the editable `.blend` project.
