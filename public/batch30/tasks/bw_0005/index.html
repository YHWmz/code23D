We're handling the interior fit-out for this Montessori classroom, and I need a render to walk the owner and site supervisor through the layout at next month's progress review — clean enough that we can add markups on it. Please follow the room in the image: a bright rectangular space with a full row of large windows along the back wall for daylight, light walls and a light wood floor. Estimate the ceiling height from the image proportions, and don't change the layout. The main pieces are several waist-high open wooden low cabinets and shelves running along the walls and through the middle of the room; on the shelves, lay out Montessori materials loosely with trays, small wooden boxes and geometric blocks — no need to match every item. Put one or two light-wood activity tables with child-sized stools in the center and a round rug on the floor, and fill in any other usual furnishings within the reference image. Keep all the wood a warm natural tone with a matte grain, cream walls, light wood flooring; size everything so everything stays in scale with kids. The key thing is that the overall layout and how the furniture relates reads at a glance, with the main pieces not buried under clutter, so it's easy to follow and leaves room for callouts. Use daylight from the windows as the key light — warm-white, soft, slightly directional — plus a soft even ambient fill for a bright, airy feel. Put the camera in a corner and shoot a slightly wide-angle panorama covering most of the layout. Render with Cycles at 1920×1080, sampling and denoising tuned clean, and hand over the saved.blend too.
