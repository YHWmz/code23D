Art & Animation / Animation, Motion & Art

Query · 原始任务

I teach an animation class and want a short demo clip for my slides that shows how a mechanical linkage drives a whole sequence of motion — done as a retro-industrial kinetic-typography piece I can break down step by step. An old brass-and-steel machine picks up, flips and assembles scattered 3D letters one by one into the word MOMENTUM, then locks them into a title card. The mechanism is really the point, so students need to follow it layer by layer: a same-module gear train (a 400mm main gear plus several 60–200mm driven gears meshing correctly), two camshafts driving reciprocating push rods, a 2m conveyor feeding in the letter blanks, and two three-joint robotic arms doing the pick-and-place. The gear rotation, rod strokes and arm motions have to sync up in time so I can pause and explain each stage. Letters are bold sans-serif, 0.5m tall, extruded 0.15m with an 8mm bevel, ending up evenly spaced in a single row; the base is a 4m×1.4m dark wooden bench, with rails, rivets and similar retro-industrial details. Materials: brass worn and lightly oxidized, steel in cool grey with clear-coat highlights, matte wood. Warm workshop lighting — a key with a cool rim and ambient fill, plus an interior HDRI. Camera pushes in low then slowly orbits, 35mm, shallow depth of field. Main thing: the drive chain mustn't block itself and each part should stay clearly separable for teaching. Render in Cycles at 1920×1080, 25fps, 8 seconds (200 frames), with adaptive sampling and motion blur; give me a PNG sequence plus a composited MP4, and save the project as a.blend so I can tweak it later.
